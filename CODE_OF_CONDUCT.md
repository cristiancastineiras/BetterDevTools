# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We want BetterDevTools to be a welcoming, respectful and harassment-free project for everyone, regardless of experience, identity, background or ability.

## Expected behaviour

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Discuss ideas and code, not personal characteristics.
- Accept feedback without hostility.
- Respect privacy and avoid publishing personal or sensitive information.
- Help create an environment where people can participate safely.

## Unacceptable behaviour

- Harassment, threats, insults or discriminatory language.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption or repeated bad-faith arguments.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a concern.

## Scope

This code applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions and other project spaces. It also applies when someone publicly represents BetterDevTools.

## Reporting

Report conduct concerns privately to the repository maintainer through their GitHub profile:

<https://github.com/cristiancastineiras>

Do not include sensitive details in a public issue. Reports will be reviewed as privately and promptly as reasonably possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict or permanently ban participation when behaviour violates this code. Enforcement decisions will consider the context, severity and recurrence of the conduct.
